THE CROSSING — THE WHOLE CREATURE BOOK
31 sheets. Nothing in this region has a sprite; the mountain's 43 do.
Six poses each, 3x2 at 1536x1024 — the shipped pattern:
    idle · its own pose · move-a · move-b · attack · death
The four birds are eight, because four of their frames go on flight
(up down glide landing) and at six that costs them the death frame — the
exact fault the hill's birds were redone at eight to fix.

HOW TO USE ONE
  1  copy a prompt
  2  save what comes back as       output/crossing-mobs/<id>/source.png
  3  run the cut line printed above the prompt
  4  node scripts/build-mob-strips.mjs <id> --patch
  5  node scripts/audit-mob-strips.mjs        (must pass)
  6  node scripts/build-mob-preview.mjs       and LOOK at it
No ART_V bump: every one of these is a new filename.

WHAT ORDER TO DO THEM IN
  The six that carry a route, if you only want to see it working first:
    wrack-crab (16 rooms) · great-gull (11) · ford-eel (11) · conger (8)
    oystercatcher (6) · marsh-hound (6)
  Then the other commons, then the seven rare forms, then the three bosses.

ELEVEN OF THESE ARE DEAD, AND THE PROSE NEVER SAYS SO
  "It walks the causeway from end to end with a notched stick in one hand" does
  not tell anyone the tide warden drowned two centuries ago. The prompts now say
  it, read straight off the game's own HOLLOW and GRAVE_FLESH sets so a sheet
  cannot disagree with the tables that decide whether the thing bleeds.
  ELEVEN DEAD, EXACTLY TWO LIVING (the strand thief and the wrecker).
  the-fowler was drawn as a living poacher on this mistake and needs REDOING.

THE DEAD DRAW THEIR EYES IN #00D0D0 CYAN, WHICH IS NOT THE FINAL COLOUR
  On a blood moon — one night in every 9.2 days — every hollow thing's eyes come
  up red. That cannot be a filter (CSS reddens every pixel the same) and it must
  not be a second sheet (the same individual will not come back the same, so the
  creature would change on red nights). So the eyes are drawn as a flat marker
  colour and the cutter splits them off into a tiny separate layer, the way the
  magenta background is split off. Measured: cyan cannot be keyed out, and the
  nearest pixel in all 61 shipped strips is 181 away, so nothing collides.
  It costs NO extra sheets — the marker rides in the sheet you were drawing.

THREE THINGS THAT MUST BE WIRED WHEN THE STRIPS LAND
  1 · MOB_SPRITE has no entry for any of these. Starting heights below.
  2 · CALM_POSES must gain the work poses, or they are drawn and never shown.
      Ten of these dead are still AT WORK and the work pose IS the creature —
      but a name the client does not know is unreachable for anything that has
      a gait, which is what happened to the hill's variants on 9 September.
      Add:  cut-the-stick · read-the-water · lift-the-trap · drive-the-road
            turn-from-the-wall · work-the-stone · feed-the-flue · work-the-pan
            dress-the-stone · part-the-reed · rise-from-the-turf
  3 · client ROOTED is still only the milker and the butter wife. The salt
      widow and the scaffold hand work in place too and belong in it.

ONE I DO NOT THINK YOU SHOULD DRAW
  the-quicksand is not a creature. It has no shape, nothing to hit, and the
  banned list forbids scenery — its six poses are a patch of ground doing
  things, which is the one subject this pipeline has no way to make good. Its
  prompt is at the end anyway, but it is the only sheet here I would skip and
  let stay pictureless.

TWO SHEETS THAT WILL FIGHT YOU
  the-eel-cutter works from a punt. The boat is NOT drawn — a punt fills the
  cell and the pole held upright makes the box two and a half men tall. He gets
  the pole held level across him, the way the herdsman holds his staff, and a
  wicker trap at the belt. The boat is implied.
  the-drowned-ferryman is under the water except his hands. His idle is head
  and hands only on the rope; he rises through the set. He fights at The
  Mid-Channel, which has no room plate and keeps the ferry ground, so he stands
  on the ferry plate's rope and green water.
